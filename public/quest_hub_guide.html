2. Preserved Original Vocabulary (No Truncation)

  • German Module 2 Kulturzone + Units 1 through 6: Complete lexicon intact including articles (der/die/das), plurals, gender variations, and phrase examples.
  • Cambridge Latin Book 1: Stages 1 through 12: Every single entry preserved with exact vocabulary and context.
  • Smart Latin Grader Single Pronoun Support: Does not force concatenated "he she it works". Separated pronouns such as "he works", "she works", "it works", "is working", or "works" are accepted.
  • German Slash Grammar Supports slashed forms such as die Schüler / die Schülerinnen allowing students to answer with either form.

3. Multiplayer Arena (Real-Time 6-Digit PIN)
